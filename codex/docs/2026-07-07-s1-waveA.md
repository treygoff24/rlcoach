## What changed

- Kept the cherry-picked A1 WIP that discriminates press-liftoff with `JUMP_LIFTOFF_MIN_UP_VEL_STEP`, seeds implied liftoff press candidates, fits bounded liftoff release offsets, and fixes tracker jump timers for airborne state.
- Fixed the WP7c press ruler: exact raw-frame hits are still reported, but the scored recall now maps solved `JumpPress` events into the same coalesced component-target key space as flip intervals. The tolerance is +/-2 frames, covering the observed ground-side component counter vs Air-side liftoff convention gap.
- Added a press pipeline trace for every known press target: injected frames, raw/legal press candidates, selected candidate, rejection reason, emitted raw frames, and coalesced hit.
- Added low-confidence (`0.3`, `LOW_MARGIN`) liftoff boundary `JumpPress` events for unseeded Air segments when a press-liftoff is observable near the segment boundary but no interval candidate emitted a press. These events do not cap segment confidence.
- Added A4 hallucination geometry diagnostics: position, nearest arena-plane surface margin, min other-car distance in the span, and velocity/angular deltas.
- Added A5 confidence diagnostics over solved samples, events, and segment summaries.
- Added an Air-only guard for unseeded second-action flip candidates. Kinematic dodge/double-jump candidates are no longer generated on non-Air/surface-excluded segments; seeded component paths remain unchanged.

## Remaining ambiguities

1. Whether the fixed press target should remain a tolerance-based diagnostic ruler or become an output-contract convention. The timeline event still records the raw observed event frame; the diagnostic maps it into coalesced target space.
2. Remaining quiet flips are still low-altitude/surface-adjacent. The Air-only candidate gate is safe, but a stronger segment exclusion or mechanic-specific low-altitude gate needs a design decision because it can change what the oracle counts as eligible truth.
3. Residual `KINEMATIC_GATE_DID_NOT_FIRE` and `TRACKER_SECOND_NOT_AVAILABLE` misses need a candidate-shape/span-dilution pass, likely max-over-subinterval signatures rather than another broad candidate expansion.
