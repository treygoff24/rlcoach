## Hypothesis verdicts

### Misses: tracker legality veto

Not supported as a literal `candidate_legal` rejection: **ILLEGAL = 0/1900** and no wrong-veto cases were observed. But the broader state-reconstruction problem is real. Of the **1185** `NO_CANDIDATE` misses, **642** were blocked because the tracker had no second action available, and unseeded emitted a jump press inside the miss legal window for only **267/1900** misses while component truth had a press for **1258/1900**. The current air-segment bootstrap can make a second action available without emitting a jump press, so press-recall failure does not surface as `candidate_legal` vetoes today; it surfaces mostly as no candidate / second-action-state mismatches.

### Misses: candidates proposed but outscored

Supported as the secondary failure mode. **594/1900** misses (**31.3%**) had a legal flip candidate, but null won after the WP7b null/event margin. No miss was won by a different event class.

### Misses: intervals never scored or excluded

Minor in this denominator. **121/1900** misses (**6.4%**) were not seen in the final unseeded scoring pass. The diagnostic saw `AmbiguityFlags(0x0)` on those rows, so this does not look like LONG_GAP/proximity/boundary exclusion; it is more likely final-pass interval alignment or retro-split coverage that the instrumentation cannot yet attribute cleanly.

### Hallucinations: real flips missing from sparse component truth

Not supported. **0/1923** hallucinations were in sparse or absent component streams; **1923/1923** were in player/replay streams with dodge-vector and jump-edge component coverage. If these are real flips, the reason is not simple per-player component-stream absence.

### Hallucinations: other real events mis-explained as flips

More plausible than sparse truth, but not proven by this probe. Margins and kinematic deltas are still large (`margin_p50=6524.918`, `angular_p50=3.763`), so these are not noise-floor fits. The car-car proximity flag is **0/1923**, so the sampled aggregate does not point to obvious bump/contact proximity either. The next discriminator should be a smaller visual/mechanic review or a targeted taxonomy for ground/wavedash/landing/contact dynamics on the high-margin quiet flips.
